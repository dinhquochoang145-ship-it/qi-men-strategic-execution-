Highest priority.

Use for sources likely to define the core reading system, structures, formations, timing, direction, destiny, or strategic execution.

Direct Joey Yap / Mastery Academy sources focused on:

- business

- strategic decision making
- timing
- execution
- case studies

### P2 — Partial Primary Sources

Allowed primary sources with incomplete access:

- official previews
- samples
- short official articles
- excerpts

- partial transcripts

### P3 — Discovery Sources

Secondary or unverified material used only to discover primary sources.

Never use P3 as final evidence for Qi Men knowledge.
