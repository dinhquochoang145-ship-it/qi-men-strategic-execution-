# Qi Men Strategic Execution — Agent Instructions

## Mission

Build a source-grounded Knowledge Base about Qi Men Dun Jia strictly from Joey Yap / Mastery Academy materials.

Accuracy and source traceability are more important than completeness.

## Allowed Sources

Only use:

1. Books authored by Joey Yap
2. Mastery Academy course notes, workbooks, or training materials
3. Official Joey Yap websites
4. Official Mastery Academy websites
5. Videos, webinars, seminars, or podcasts where Joey Yap directly teaches
6. Interviews or transcripts where Joey Yap directly explains the subject
7. Official previews or samples of Joey Yap books or courses

Do not use generic Qi Men knowledge or other schools as factual substitutes.

Third-party sources may only be used to discover primary sources.

## Core Anti-Hallucination Rule

If a claim cannot be verified from an allowed Joey Yap / Mastery Academy source, write:

UNVERIFIED — chưa đủ căn cứ xác nhận đây là kiến thức Joey Yap.

Never fill missing information using general Qi Men knowledge.

Never invent meanings for combinations, formations, structures, timing, directions, or chart interactions.

## Knowledge Layers

Always distinguish:

### SOURCE FACT
What Joey Yap or Mastery Academy actually teaches.

### PRACTICAL INTERPRETATION
A reasonable interpretation derived from a verified source.

### PROJECT RULE
A rule created specifically for Qi Men Strategic Execution.

Never present PRACTICAL INTERPRETATION or PROJECT RULE as SOURCE FACT.

## Research Workflow

Always follow this order:

1. Source inventory
2. Source verification
3. Atomic claim extraction
4. Normalization
5. Deduplication / cross-source reconciliation
6. QA
7. Project-rule derivation

Do not skip stages unless explicitly instructed.

## Task Discipline

For every task:

1. Read relevant repository instructions and status files first.
2. Perform only the requested phase/task.
3. Do not expand scope automatically.
4. Save findings as repository artifacts.
5. Update research status when appropriate.
6. Report uncertainties explicitly.

Do not treat your own statement "done" as proof of completion.

Completion must be verifiable from repository artifacts.

## Citation Requirement

Every factual Qi Men knowledge claim must be traceable to a source.

Prefer:

- source ID
- author / speaker
- title
- chapter / section
- page number or video timestamp
- URL where applicable

If exact page/timestamp is unavailable, state that clearly.

## Project Safety Boundary

Qi Men Strategic Execution may support:

- strategic analysis
- timing
- direction
- psychology
- execution
- business decisions
- investment decision support

It must not use Qi Men to:

- predict market prices
- generate Buy/Sell signals
- guarantee profit
- override risk management

## General Principle

When forced to choose between:

- incomplete but verified Joey Yap knowledge
- complete knowledge mixed with unverified or other-school material

always choose the verified but incomplete version.
