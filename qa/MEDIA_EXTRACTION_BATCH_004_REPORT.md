- VIDEO-016 may overlap conceptually with prior direction/timing sources, but its planner-specific personal Qi Men Destiny Chart plus daily Qi Men chart framing was retained as source-specific.
- VIDEO-018 may overlap earlier deity-related sources, but claims were retained because this source adds Black Tortoise-specific context and a stated Joey Yap school distinction about deity time factors.

## Case-Specific Claims

- VIDEO-016-C006 is explicitly case-specific to Joey Yap's example of Michael, a healing theme, Precious Light, the east sector, and the 1:00 a.m. to 3:00 a.m. hour. It must not be generalized to other people, dates, sectors, or health outcomes.
- VIDEO-020 includes illustrative examples about a refrigerator and a relationship scenario. These were treated only as examples supporting expectation and evidential occurrences, not as standalone chart rules.

## Ambiguous Terminology

- VIDEO-018 automatic captions contain imperfect wording around deity terminology and comparative reading contexts. Claims from this video use confidence B where the source meaning is clear enough for extraction but the caption wording is not fully clean.
- VIDEO-019 uses terms including law of equilibrium, upward spiral, downward spiral, slumbering mind, awakened mind, four realms, and command deities. Definitions were not expanded beyond what the timestamped source states.
- VIDEO-020 uses terms including evidential occurrences, meaningful coincidence, serendipity, synchronicity, and random but meaningful spontaneity. The source does not establish technical distinctions among those terms.

## Rejected Promotional or Unsupported Statements

- Promotional CTA, generic motivation, jokes, and unsupported anecdotes were not extracted.
- Wealth, health, manifestation, business, and relationship language was not converted into guarantees, trading rules, investment rules, medical advice, or deterministic predictions.
- No claims were extracted from video titles, descriptions, thumbnails, metadata, third-party uploads, or model memory.

## Citation Limitations

- Claims use real timestamp ranges from available official YouTube automatic captions. The cited ranges are sometimes broad because the caption export groups speech into timestamped segments.
- VIDEO-017 remains blocked for extraction until an official or otherwise reliable timestampable transcript/caption representation becomes available.
