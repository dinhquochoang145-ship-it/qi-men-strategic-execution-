## Duplicate Candidates

- WEBINAR-001 overlaps with earlier extracted media on four realms, deities, healing, Black Tortoise, and manifesting; claims were retained only where Joey Yap provided webinar-specific framing or Q&A answers.
- WEBINAR-002 overlaps with prior extracted video claims on forecasting, Rest Door, Life Door, Open Door, D/E/void, Flying Bird Fall Into Cave, and business/property cases; claims were retained as webinar-specific examples with case-specific boundaries.
- WEBINAR-003 through WEBINAR-005 may contain Team Joey Yap or coach explanations that overlap with prior manifesting content, but they were not extracted in this Joey-direct batch.

## Ambiguous Terminology

- Automatic captions frequently render Qi Men as "achievement," "chiman," "treatment," "shimon," or similar wording. Extracted claims use Qi Men terminology only where the timestamped source context supports it.
- WEBINAR-001 and WEBINAR-002 use some caption-rendered terms such as "D E," "Grain," "Envoy Rest," and "Flying Bird Fall Into Cave"; these were preserved with confidence B where caption wording is imperfect.
- Several WEBINAR-002 claims are case-specific health/property/business readings and must remain case-specific until later normalization and reconciliation.

## Promotional or Unsupported Material Rejected

- Webinar setup, greetings, registration copy, event logistics, pricing, installment details, countdowns, CTA language, and course-sales sections were skipped.
- Jokes, casual chatter, generic motivation, and unsupported anecdotes were not converted into SOURCE FACT.
- Health, wealth, business, investment, relationship, and manifestation language was not converted into guarantees, trading signals, medical advice, investment advice, or project rules.
- No claims were extracted from titles, descriptions, thumbnails, metadata, third-party uploads, or model memory.

## Citation Limitations

- No official human-edited transcript was used.
- Claims cite real timestamp ranges from official YouTube English (Original) automatic captions.
- Some timestamp ranges are broad because the caption export groups speech into overlapping segments and the recordings are long.
