2. Do not mark a source VERIFIED merely because its title mentions Joey Yap.
3. Verification must establish provenance from an allowed source category in AGENTS.md.
4. Third-party sources remain SECONDARY even if their content appears accurate.
5. If provenance cannot be established, use UNVERIFIED.
6. Do not record Qi Men interpretations or knowledge claims in this file.
7. Duplicate editions or mirrors should reference the canonical source in Notes instead of being treated as independent evidence.
